How to File a Claim
Collect the provider bill, receipt, treatment date, procedure details, and member information.
Confirm that the claim information matches your coverage and provider records.
Submit the claim through the designated claims portal or approved paper process.
Keep a copy of the submitted documents and note the submission date.
Review the claim status periodically. Typical statuses include Pending, Approved, and Denied.
Claim Review
The claims team reviews submitted information, verifies eligibility and coverage, and determines the applicable member responsibility.
If a Claim Is Denied
Review the denial explanation and follow the plan's appeal or reconsideration instructions. Submit supporting documentation when requested.
